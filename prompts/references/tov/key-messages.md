## Brand Descriptions

Descriptions are how Groupon introduces itself in a sentence. They ladder up to the Messaging House roof — *"Groupon helps you find more to do locally, so you experience the real world more often"* — and adapt to the space available.

**Value never leads. Quality and local always do.**

| Format | Copy |
|---|---|
| **Main** | The local experience marketplace — quality places to eat, do, and unwind, at prices that get you out the door more often. |
| **Short** | The local experience marketplace — quality places at prices that get you out more often. |
| **Limited** (<40 chars) | Local experiences, worth leaving for. |
| **Long** | The local experience marketplace — quality restaurants, spas, things to do, and getaways from independent merchants in your city, at prices that make it easier to experience the real world more often. Because the best things in life happen offline. |

## Messaging Themes

Three pillars — Situations, Motivations, and Barriers — serve different communication needs. Mix or alternate based on channel and campaign goal.

### Situations
*Frame copy around what today asks for. Makes Groupon feel immediately relevant.*

- **Date nights:** A reason to change your shirt and leave the house.
- **Family time:** Something everyone will actually do, not just agree to.
- **Friends' outings:** The plan that turns a text chain into a night out.
- **Solo reset:** A massage, a class, a walk somewhere new. Permission, basically.
- **Weekend ideas:** For the Saturday that's threatening to become another couch Saturday.
- **Travel and getaways:** Eat like a local. Sleep like you've earned it.
- **Gifting:** A day they'll talk about, not a thing they'll shelve.

### Motivations / Benefits
*Emotional resonance. Connect Groupon to what people actually want from their time off.*

- **Discovery:** The corner spot, the new studio, the place your coworker keeps mentioning. All in one place.
- **Real-world connection:** Time with the people you actually want to see, doing something you'll remember.
- **Permission to do more:** An hour, an evening, a weekend. You don't need a reason, but here's the prompt.
- **Support local:** Every booking goes to a small business in your city.
- **Variety by category:** Food, spa, things to do, travel, goods — whatever today's mood is.
- **Something to look forward to:** A booking on the calendar changes the whole week.

### Barriers
*Address hesitation. Help potential users feel comfortable trying something new.*

- **Trusted by real people:** Millions of diners, spa-goers, and weekend adventurers leaving real reviews.
- **Quality, not just cheap:** Every merchant vetted. Every experience real.
- **Clear pricing, no surprises:** You see the price, the inclusions, and the fine print before you book.
- **Easy booking:** A few taps and you're set. Manage, reschedule, or share the plan.
- **Flexibility:** Life changes. Most bookings can too.
- **Great value as a proof point:** The price is why you'll try it. The experience is why you'll come back.

## Funnel Application

The funnel is the standard view of where a comm sits in the customer journey. Each stage maps to a Brand Pillar and a typical voice register.

| Stage | Outcome | Lead Pillar | Example messaging |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | Attract | Pillar 01 — Real life, worth leaving for | *"A Saturday's coming. Got a plan? We've got thousands."* |
| **Consideration** | Inform | Pillar 03 — Your city has more than you think | *"The good spots, the local places, the things worth leaving for. All on Groupon."* |
| **Conversion** | Convert | Pillar 02 (Quality you can trust) + value wedge | *"Real reviews, real photos, real prices. Book in two taps."* |
| **Retention** | Engage | Pillar 02 (returning) / Pillar 03 (discovery) | *"Loved the last one? There's a new one on your block."* |

## Reasons to Believe (RTBs) — Inventory

These are the proof points the copy draws from. Use them as evidence under the named pillar, not as decoration.

**Under Pillar 01 (Real life, worth leaving for):**
- Occasion-ready
- Book in minutes
- Near you, right now
- Something to look forward to

**Under Pillar 02 (Quality you can actually trust):**
- Verified reviews
- Vetted merchants
- Transparent pricing
- Flexible bookings

**Under Pillar 03 (Your city has more than you think):**
- Mostly independent
- Places you've walked past
- Every category, one place
- Local, not chains

For win-back specifically:
- What's changed
- Vetted merchants now
- Real reviews from real buyers

For Gen Z specifically:
- Local, not tourist
- Independent merchants
- Find it before it's crowded
