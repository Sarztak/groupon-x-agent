# Funnel Application

> **Source:** B2C TOV v4.6 (April 2026) — Section: "Funnel application"
> **Brand Pillars sourced from:** Messaging House v1 — Brand Pillars 01, 02, 03

The funnel is the standard view of where a comm sits in the customer journey. Each stage maps to a Brand Pillar and a typical voice register. This expands on the Messaging House by adding pillar attribution.

---

## The Funnel Table

| Stage | Outcome | Lead Pillar | Example messaging |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | Attract | Pillar 01 — Real life, worth leaving for | *"A Saturday's coming. Got a plan? We've got thousands."* |
| **Consideration** | Inform | Pillar 03 — Your city has more than you think | *"The good spots, the local places, the things worth leaving for. All on Groupon."* |
| **Conversion** | Convert | Pillar 02 (Quality you can trust) + value wedge | *"Real reviews, real photos, real prices. Book in two taps."* |
| **Retention** | Engage | Pillar 02 (returning) / Pillar 03 (discovery) | *"Loved the last one? There's a new one on your block."* |

---

## How to Use This

When a brief identifies the funnel stage but not the lead pillar, this table is the lookup.

When a brief identifies the lead pillar but not the funnel stage, this table tells you what stage that pillar is best suited for — useful for sanity-checking whether the comm's job and the lead pillar match.

When a brief is multi-funnel (e.g. a CRM newsletter that does awareness + retention), pick the dominant job for the hero spot. Secondary modules can run a different pillar — but don't try to do all four stages in one comm.

---

## Funnel vs. Audience Segment

These two lenses are complementary but distinct:

- **Funnel stage** answers: *where is the user in the journey?*
- **Audience segment** answers: *what kind of person is the user?*

A piece of conversion-stage CRM to Lapsed Believers will lead with Pillar 02 (both lenses agree). A piece of awareness-stage paid social to Value Hunters has a tension — funnel says Pillar 01, audience says Value wedge + Pillar 02. In tensions, **audience segment usually wins** because it's the more enduring fact about the reader; the funnel stage is contextual.
