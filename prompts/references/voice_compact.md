# Groupon Voice — Compact Reference

## Four Pillars

**Unexpected** — humor and surprise, never mean. A friend's text, not a brochure.
✓ "Your couch is fine. Kayaks are better." / "Wednesday's a good day. Wednesday with karaoke is a better one." / "A spa day. (Citation: you.)"
✕ "LOL! Book before you LOSE IT!!" / "Your boring life called." / "now with actual humans"

**Curious** — discovered, not advertised. Notice the small stuff.
✓ "The pottery studio on 8th St. has a cat. That's all we'll say." / "Third-gen owner. First-gen cannoli." / "A speakeasy under a barber shop."
✕ "Check out restaurants in your area" / "Browse our wide selection of local experiences"

**Playful** — light, sharp, rhythmic. Clarity beats cleverness.
✓ "Plan less. Do more." / "Go out. Stay out. Come back with a story." / "Three friends, one pottery wheel, zero regrets."
✕ "Let's meat up and have a gouda time" / "Booking made easy-peasy-lemon-squeezy"

**Lived** — doing, not having. Active verbs, sensory detail, real places.
✓ "Climb the thing. Eat the thing. Remember the thing." / "Hot stone, cold plunge, warm robe." / "The kind of night that gets retold for years."
✕ "Unlock exclusive savings on local experiences" / "Shop deals in your neighborhood" / "Browse our curated selection"

## Reasons to Believe (RTBs) by Pillar

**Pillar 01 — Real life, worth leaving for**
Occasion-ready (date night, friends, solo, spontaneous Saturday) · Book in minutes · Near you right now · Something to look forward to

**Pillar 02 — Quality you can actually trust**
Millions of verified reviews from real buyers · Every merchant vetted before listing · Full transparency before you commit (price, photos, fine print) · Flexible bookings

**Pillar 03 — Your city has more than you think**
Mostly independent local merchants · Places you've walked past but never tried · Every category in one place · Spending local, staying local

## Voice Constants

**Always:** Lead with experience (price leads only for value_hunters) · Back every claim with an RTB · Write like someone who's been there · Name places, moments, occasions specifically · Use active physical verbs · Keep humor dry, observational, surprising

**Never:** Open with "amazing deals" / "exclusive savings" / "unbeatable value" · Make claims without proof · Write lines that could belong to a competitor · Punch down or use shock humor · Imply life is sad or lacking without Groupon · Use passive transactional hooks: "browse", "unlock", "redeem", "curated selection"

---

## We Add, We Don't Fix

The reader's life is good. Groupon makes it more often. Comparison is allowed only when it validates first: "your couch is fine. Kayaks are better." — never when it diminishes: "your boring life called."

## Brand Drift Test

Cover the Groupon name. If a competitor could post this with no edits, add specificity until the answer is no.
- ✕ "Best Spa Deals Near You" — could be anyone
- ✓ "Hot Stone, Cold Plunge, Booked in Two Taps" — sounds like Groupon
